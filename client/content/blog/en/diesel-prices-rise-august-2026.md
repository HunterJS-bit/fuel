Euro diesel prices across Serbia rose an average of **1.2%** in the third week of August 2026, according to data from over 500 stations.

## Key changes

- **NIS Petrol** and **OMV** raised prices in Belgrade and Novi Sad
- **Eco** kept the lowest capital average at **194 RSD/L**
- Southern regions saw slightly larger increases

Analysts point to higher crude prices and pre-holiday demand.
