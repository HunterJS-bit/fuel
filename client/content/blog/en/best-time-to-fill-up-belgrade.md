Six months of data show **Tuesday & Wednesday 6–9 AM** as the cheapest fill-up window in Belgrade.

- Fridays and Saturdays trend higher
- Highway-exit stations cost **3–5 RSD/L** more
- Filling **50 L on Tuesday vs Saturday** saves ~**150 RSD/month**
