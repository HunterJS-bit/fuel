**NIS Petrol** rolled out a mid-month adjustment across all **312 stations**.

| Fuel | Change |
|------|--------|
| Euro Diesel | +0.5 RSD/L |
| BMB 95 | +0.3 RSD/L |

International benchmarks and FX moves were cited as the main drivers. Loyalty card holders may still get discounts at select sites.
